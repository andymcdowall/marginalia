# BYLAWS OF THE UNIVERSITY OF NORTH TEXAS ALUMNI ASSOCIATION, Inc.

## ARTICLE I – NAME

The organization shall be known as The University of North Texas Alumni Association, Inc., herein referred to as the “UNTAA” or “the Association.”

## ARTICLE II – PURPOSE

The purpose of the Association is to support the University of North Texas (“University” or “UNT”) by serving as the mechanism to engage alumni and friends in the life of the University by (a) establishing and maintaining positive, quality relationships between the University and its alumni; (b) encouraging graduates, former students, and friends of the University to promote and advance the mission and priorities of the University; and (c) promoting the general welfare and interests of the University through the provision of alumni service to the University as approved by the University.

## ARTICLE III – OFFICE

The principal office of the Association, also the registered office, will be 801 North Texas Blvd, Suite 170, Denton, TX 76201. The office of the registered agent of the Association, the Executive Director, is the same as the principal office and the registered office of the Association.

## ARTICLE IV – BOARD OF DIRECTORS

- The Board of Directors of the Association is vested with the management of the business and affairs of the Association, as approved by the University, and as subject to these by-laws.

- The Board shall be comprised of no more than 21 Directors-At-Large, the Executive Committee members and the Association’s Executive Director.

- The voting members of the Board shall be the Officers/Executive Committee Members and the Directors-At-Large.

- Board member terms are three (3) years in length. At the end of a board member’s first term, the board member is eligible for consideration to be re-elected to the Board for a second three (3) year term.

- Board Members may serve a maximum of two (2) consecutive three (3) year terms, if elected. Upon completion of two (2) consecutive terms, a board member must leave the Board for a minimum of three (3) years before eligible for re-election.

- Board member terms will be staggered with a maximum of seven (7) board members in each class with a total of 21 Directors-At-Large at any given time.

- Elections to the Board of Directors are the responsibility of the Association’s Executive Director. Nominations will be sought throughout the year and will be discussed within the Association’s Executive Committee for presentation to the full board for vote at the last Regular meeting of the fiscal year. Nominations/suggestions must be received by the Office of Alumni Relations at least 14 days in advance of a Regular meeting for consideration by the Executive Director and Executive Committee.

- Any Director may resign at any time by delivering written notice to the Executive Director. Such resignation shall take effect upon receipt.

- Any Director may be removed without cause, at any time, by a majority vote of the Executive Committee as approved by the Executive Director.

- In the event of a vacancy of any member of the Board of Directors, the Executive Director will recommend to the Board for approval a replacement to fill the vacancy and complete the unexpired term, if any, at a Regular Board meeting.

- Directors shall not receive any compensation for their services. The Association shall not loan money or property to, or guarantee the obligation of, any Director.

- The President of the University, the Vice President of University Advancement and the Association’s Executive Director shall be non-voting, ex-officio members of the Board of Directors.

- The Board of Directors may elect a student representative to be a non-voting ex-officio member of the Board for a one-year term.

## ARTICLE VI – CODE OF CONDUCT

The Association and its Directors will comply with the Association’s Ethics Policy and Memorandum of Understanding (MOU), attached as Appendix.

## ARTICLE VII – BOARD MEETINGS

  - The Association’s Board of Directors shall hold three (3) Regular Meetings annually to be determined by the Executive Committee.

  - Special Meetings of the Board of Directors may be called by the Chairman with approval from the Alumni Relations Office/Executive Director.

  - Notice of the date, time, and place of Regular Meetings shall be given by the Alumni Relations Office to each board member by regular mail, telephone (including voicemail), or email no less than 14 days prior to the meeting. Notice of the date, time, and place of Special Meetings shall be given by the Alumni Relations Office using the same methods, but with no less than seven days prior to the meeting.

- One meeting per year shall be open to the public. Preference will be given to the 2nd meeting.

- Board members shall notify the Office of Alumni Relations a minimum of four (4) days prior to a Regular meeting for an excused absence. Excessive absences from board meetings, as determined by the Executive Committee, shall be cause for consideration for dismissal from the board.

- A majority of the incumbent Directors (not counting vacancies) shall constitute a quorum for the purposes of convening a meeting or conducting business. At Board meetings where a quorum is present, a majority vote of the Directors attending shall constitute an act of the Board. If a Director must leave a meeting prior to a vote, a proxy may be left with the Chairman or the Executive Director.

- The proceedings and business of the Board of Directors shall be governed by Robert’s Rules of Parliamentary Procedure/Robert’s Rules of Order Newly Revised in cases to which they are applicable unless otherwise provided herein.

## ARTICLE VIII – OFFICERS

- The officers of the Board of Directors shall be Chairman, Vice Chairman, Secretary, Treasurer and Immediate Past Chairman. The officers will serve as counsel to the Executive Director in the oversight of the general affairs of the Association including financial matters.

- Terms of office shall coincide with the University’s fiscal year of September 1 – August 31.

- The Chairman shall not serve more than two (2) consecutive one (1) year terms of office (except the unexpired term of his/her predecessor, if any); shall preside at meetings of the Board and the Association; shall appoint standing committee members; shall provide ambassadorial support to the activities of the Association in carrying out its objectives, goals, and policies; and perform other duties as may be assigned by the Board of Directors.

- The Vice Chairman shall not serve more than two (2) consecutive one (1) year terms of office; shall preside at all meetings of the Board and Association from which the Chairman is absent; shall provide ambassadorial support to the activities of the Association and serve as “Chair-Elect.”

- The Secretary shall not serve more than two (2) consecutive one (1) year terms of office; shall keep a record of attendance of all Board meetings; and shall serve as a backup to the Alumni Relations staff in regard to the recording of meeting minutes; and shall provide ambassadorial support to the activities of the Association.

- The Treasurer shall not serve more than two (2) consecutive one (1) year terms of office; shall serve as counsel to the Executive Director in the Association’s financial matters; and shall provide ambassadorial support to the activities of the Association.

- The Executive Director will seek the counsel of the Executive Committee in presenting a slate of new officers to the full Board for a vote/election for the office of Chairman, Vice Chairman, Secretary and Treasurer at the last Regular meeting of the fiscal year.

- In the event of a vacancy of any officer other than Chairman, the Executive Committee will recommend to the Board for approval, a replacement to fill the vacancy and complete the unexpired term, at a Regular or Special meeting. In the event that the Chairman is unable to fulfill his/her term, the Vice Chairman shall assume his/her duties and serve out the remaining term.

## ARTICLE IX– ASSOCIATION MEMBERSHIP

- Association membership shall consist of any graduate, former student or friend of the University of North Texas (or any of its predecessor names) who pays annual Association membership dues or who purchases a “life membership.”

- Membership dues/rates are set by the Office of Alumni Relations as approved by the Association’s Board of Directors and may be subject to change.

- Association membership rights are established by the Association, in conjunction with the Office of Alumni Relations and may be subject to change. Membership shall not include voting rights or election rights for the Association Board or the Association, except as otherwise specified herein. Association membership does not confer membership on the Association Board or entitle an Association member to attend Board meetings other than those open to the public.

## ARTICLE X– MANAGEMENT

- The fiscal year of the Association shall commence September 1 and end August 31 as aligned with the fiscal year of the University.

- The Executive Director will develop a schedule of alumni programming and an annual budget for the operations of the Association to be endorsed by the Board of Directors.

- The Association’s funds will be subject to review and auditing by the Alumni Executive Committee, the Alumni Finance & Investment Committee, and the UNT audit system.

- The Association’s funds will consist of restricted and unrestricted categories. All restricted gifts will be applied toward the donor’s request.

- It is the responsibility of the Executive Director to execute the Association’s budget as endorsed by the Board of Directors.

- UNT serves as the fiscal agent for the Association.

- The fiscal agent will disburse funds as approved in the annual budget while taking proper vouchers for such disbursement. The fiscal agent will report to the Board as requested a proper account of all transactions. The fiscal agent will function according to the policies of the Board in regard to Association’s funds. The Board may also elect to seek an outside financial advisor, and when appropriate, compensate that individual for their services. The fiscal agent will keep in a permanent record belonging to the Association full and accurate account of all receipts and disbursements related to such assets. The fiscal agent will deposit monies, securities or other valuable effects in the name of the Association in such depositories as may be designated for the purposes of the Association.

- The Executive Director of the Alumni Association shall be a regular member of the staff of The University of North Texas and, thus, will be selected in the usual manner prescribed by the University; shall be responsible for the operation of the Office of Alumni Relations and its functions; shall have general supervision over the activities of the Association while carrying out the objectives, goals, and policies of the Association; shall be responsible for securing space for meetings, notifying members, and shall attend all meetings of the Board of Directors and serve as advisory to the Board. The Executive Director shall oversee Board education and assessments.

- The Association shall indemnify and hold harmless its directors and officers against all liabilities and expenses in any proceeding arising out of their status and activities as directors or officers provided they were at the time acting in the best interest of and on behalf of the Association; the Association shall not indemnify a director or officer against liabilities or expenses that such person may incur attendant to activities that at the time taken were known or believed to be in conflict with the best interest of the Association.

- Management of the Association shall be consistent with all applicable laws, regulations, Association policies and University policies pertaining to Affiliated Entities.

## ARTICLE XI– CHARTERED CHAPTERS

The Association will approve and recognize alumni chapters as defined and led by the Office of Alumni Relations. These chapters will serve as fundamental infrastructure to build and maintain alumni outreach programming to alumni in geographic regions with a minimum critical mass of alumni population or by affinity group as approved by the Office of Alumni Relations.

- A Chartered Chapter of the UNTAA must have established by-laws and charter approved by the UNTAA Board of Directors and the Executive Director of the Alumni Association.

- A Chartered Chapter must have a slate of officers as approved by the Office of Alumni Relations consisting of, but not limited to, a President, Vice President and Secretary.

## ARTICLE XII–CHAPTER AFFILIATES

Requests by alumni constituent groups to establish chapter affiliates must meet the following criteria for consideration by the Board of Directors:

- The constituent group must be affiliated with the University of North Texas in an academic or geographic nature or by special interest to the university.

- The constituent group must have a critical mass of committed alumni and be deemed sustainable by the Office of Alumni Relations and adhere to a standard set of by-laws established by the Association.

## ARTICLE XIII – ASSOCIATION SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM

The Association will provide scholarship awards to UNT students on an annual basis to assist the University in building a strong legacy of alumni support. The Office of Alumni Relations will provide the mechanism of collecting applications and managing/coordinating with appropriate university partners to meet established university deadlines, policies and procedures, etc. Selection criteria, award amount and renewal status will be in accordance with gift agreements and all applicable law.

## ARTICLE XIII– AMENDMENTS

The bylaws may be amended by the vote of a majority of those directors present at any Regular or Special meeting of the Board of Directors at which a quorum is present and the substance of the proposed amendment(s) has been brought to the attention of the Board of Directors at least fourteen (14) days prior to the meeting and as approved by the Office of Alumni Relations.

## ARTICLE XIV – MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS

- In the event of dissolution, the residual assets of the Association/corporation shall be turned over to the University.

- Notwithstanding any other provisions of these articles, this corporation will not carry on any other activities not permitted to be carried on by a corporation exempt from federal income tax under section 501(c)3 of the Internal Revenue Code or the corresponding provision of any future United States internal revenue law or any other corresponding provision of any future United States internal revenue law.

- The purposes for which the corporation is organized are exclusively charitable and educational within the meaning of section 501(c)3 of the Internal Revenue Code and in general to aid and assist the university.

- No part of the net earnings of the corporation shall inure to the benefit of, or be distributable to, its members, directors, officers, or other persons except that the corporation shall be authorized and empowered to pay reasonable compensation for services rendered and to make payments and distributions, in furtherance of the exempt purposes of the corporation.
